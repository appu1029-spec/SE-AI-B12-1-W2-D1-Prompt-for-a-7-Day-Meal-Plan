- Prepare idli/dosa batter and soak adai ingredients ahead; soaking and fermentation are additional unattended preparation time.
- Pressure-cook legumes in advance. Refrigerate promptly in small portions and freeze portions intended for later in the week.
- Make chutneys with tomato, onion, coriander, and spices; thicken kootu with cooked dal instead of coconut.
- Use modest measured oil, such as approximately 1 teaspoon per person per cooked dish. Substitute available gourds, greens, cabbage, or carrots in the same quantities.
- Check ingredients in oils, batter, spice mixes, and dairy products, and prevent allergen cross-contact through utensils and preparation surfaces; ingredient exclusions cannot establish cross-contact safety. FoodAllergy.org
